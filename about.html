About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a simple online blood donor registration portal designed to connect blood donors with people who need blood.

🩸 Our Purpose

The main purpose of BloodConnect is to make it easier to register blood donors and find suitable donors based on blood group and city.

❤️ How It Helps

Donor information can be searched quickly, helping users identify available donors when they need assistance.

🔒 Simple & Private

This project stores donor information locally in the browser. It does not require a separate server or database.

How BloodConnect Works

  1. Register as a blood donor.
  2. Your information is stored in the browser.
  3. Search for donors using blood group and city.
  4. View registered donors from the dashboard.
  5. Edit or delete donor information when required.
